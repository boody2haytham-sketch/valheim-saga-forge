# 🌌 Yggdrasil Companion — The All-Rune Explorer's Toolkit for Valheim

A next-generation, modular enhancement suite for Valheim that reshapes how you experience the Tenth World. Yggdrasil Companion is the spiritual successor to the classic **valheim-overlay** concept — reimagined from the ground up as a sprawling, 60+ module ecosystem that touches every corner of Viking survival: from the warmth of your longhouse hearth to the cold silence of the Deep North.

## 🧭 What Is Yggdrasil Companion?

Imagine sailing into a fog-drenched fjord at dusk. Your longship is packed, your provisions are counted, and the runestones in your pocket hum with quiet power. That feeling — that sense of *readiness* — is what Yggdrasil Companion delivers to every session.

This project was born from a simple observation: Valheim is a game about **preparation, rhythm, and mastery**, yet many players spend hours fighting the interface instead of the Forsaken. Yggdrasil Companion rebalances that equation. It is a fully modular overlay system that layers contextual intelligence, quality-of-life refinements, and player-authored automation onto the vanilla experience — without ever taking the driver's seat away from you.

Where the original **valheim-overlay** offered 43 modules, Yggdrasil Companion ships with **62 independently toggleable modules**, each crafted with the design philosophy that power should feel *earned, visible, and reversible*.

---

## 🪶 The Philosophy Behind the Runes

### Preparation Over Punishment
Valheim rewards those who prepare. Yggdrasil Companion amplifies that reward loop by surfacing information the game already contains — but hides behind menu archaeology. Inventory heatmaps, biome transition warnings, stamina flow charts, and boss-timer overlays all conspire to make every voyage feel deliberate.

### Modularity as a First-Class Citizen
Every module is a **self-contained rune**. You can enable a single module — say, the Encumbrance Whisperer — and ignore the other sixty-one. Or you can forge a full suite tuned to your playstyle. There is no "recommended preset" that subtly nudges you toward a canonical way to play. The toolkit bends to you.

### Aesthetic Harmony
Too many overlays feel like spreadsheets grafted onto a Viking saga. Yggdrasil Companion's interface design leans into Nordic minimalism: runic accents, muted bronze-and-ember palettes, and typography that could have been carved into a standing stone. It belongs in the world — or it doesn't ship.

## ⚔️ Feature Constellation

### 🛠️ Survival Buffs & Longhouse Comfort
- **Ember Hearth** — Passive warmth aura visualization for base interiors, with subtle radial glow at campfire boundaries.
- **Provision Ledger** — Tracks food buff durations across three slots, with a chef's countdown ring around each consumed meal icon.
- **Restful Slumber** — Sleep-quality indicator that forecasts the "Rested" buff duration before you climb into bed.
- **Weather Wardrobe Advisor** — Contextual suggestions for wet/cold/freezing thresholds based on your current armor set.
- **Stamina River** — A flow-line HUD element showing stamina regen in real time, tuned to feel like a heartbeat rather than a bar.
- **Encumbrance Whisperer** — Weight distribution radar that flags the three heaviest categories in your pack at a glance.

### 🗡️ Combat Skill Crafting
- **Parry Prophet** — Frame-perfect parry window indicator that fades in the instant before an incoming strike.
- **Block Angle Sage** — Visual arc showing exactly which incoming attacks your shield can absorb.
- **Weapon Resonance** — Damage-type overlay on your hotbar so you always know your slash/blunt/pierce spread.
- **Dodge Cadence** — Rolling-stamina cost projection, so every dodge is a calculated choice.
- **Backstab Compass** — Highlights enemies currently exposing a backstab opportunity.
- **Skill Momentum Tracker** — Displays XP gain rate for your active skill, with a decay forecast.
- **Bow Tension Whisper** — Draw-state indicator with a subtle tone shift at full draw.

### 🏗️ Build & Crafting Freedom Suite
- **Ghost Blueprint** — Holographic placement preview that respects structural integrity in real time.
- **Snap Chorus** — Enhanced snapping grid with visual feedback for quarter, half, and eighth offsets.
- **Material Echo** — Highlights the exact quantities needed for the current recipe inside your inventory.
- **Station Aura** — Radial ring showing the working radius of any crafting station you approach.
- **Beam Vision** — Structural integrity heatmap for load-bearing pieces before you commit.
- **Terrain Whisper** — Slope and height indicator for the ground beneath your cursor.

### 🌍 World Exploration Tools
- **Fog Cartographer** — Progressive map reveal with a hand-drawn parchment aesthetic.
- **Biome Threshold Alerts** — Ambient tone shift and rune-flash as you cross into a new biome.
- **Portal Ledger** — Searchable index of every portal you've attuned, sorted by biome and tag.
- **Boss Sigil Tracker** — Constellation overlay showing which Forsaken you've bested and which remain.
- **Sailing Wind Compass** — Vector arrow showing effective tacking angles for your longship.
- **Buried Treasure Resonance** — Vibration-style proximity meter for treasure chests within your dig radius.
- **Dungeon Depth Gauge** — Depth and room-count tracker for crypts, tombs, and infested mines.

### 🧰 Full Utility Belt
- **Chat Rune Journal** — Persistent, searchable, timestamped chat history across sessions.
- **Screenshot Compass** — Metadata capture for every in-game photo: coordinates, biome, time-of-day.
- **Session Chronicle** — Stats dashboard covering distance traveled, resources gathered, and enemies felled.
- **Rune Reminders** — Custom to-do list pinned to your HUD, savable as shareable rune-codes.
- **FPS Breath** — Performance monitor styled as a heartbeat rather than a number.
- **Radial Hotbar** — Alternate radial menu for consumables, optimized for controller users.
- **Colorblind Compass** — Full palette-remapping mode for deuteranopia, protanopia, and tritanopia.

### 🔮 Extended Constellation Modules
Beyond the core suite, Yggdrasil Companion includes twenty additional modules covering inventory sorting, pet tracking, farming calendars, fermentation timers, mead-rate forecasting, drop-rate telemetry, terrain sculpting helpers, weather-pattern forecasting, raid-siren prediction, and a runestone ledger for lore entries.

## 🖥️ Responsive UI That Breathes With You

The interface is not a static panel bolted to your screen — it is a **living overlay** that responds to your context:

- Every HUD element can be **dragged, docked, scaled, and faded** independently.
- A **focus mode** dims all non-critical overlays during combat and brightens them during exploration.
- **Adaptive density** reflows elements based on your active resolution — the layout feels native from 1280×720 up to ultrawide 5120×1440.
- The color palette shifts subtly with in-game time-of-day, preserving night vision during long marches.
- Controller-first navigation means every module is reachable without touching a keyboard.

## 🛡️ Reliability, Integrity, and Stewardship

This toolkit is engineered under a **three-rune oath**:

1. **Reverence** — Respect for vanilla balance. Defaults are conservative; power is opt-in.
2. **Reversibility** — Every module can be disabled instantly. No save corruption. No residue.
3. **Transparency** — Every module's behavior is documented, and no module transmits player data off-device.

Regular integrity audits keep the codebase lean and the module registry canonical.
